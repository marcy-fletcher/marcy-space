# Marcy Space Market

Marcy Space presents fictional digital people as if they were real and offers them through a marketplace.

## Language

**Digital Persona**:
A fictional adult character represented with realistic personal, social, and economic traits. It never corresponds to a real human.
_Avoid_: Person, product, skin

**Market Listing**:
A marketplace offer that references one Digital Persona and contains only sale-specific information such as its owner and asking price.
_Avoid_: Product, profile

**Persona Gallery**:
The image and video collection that represents a Digital Persona. It belongs to the persona rather than its Market Listing.
_Avoid_: Listing media, product gallery

**Persona Code**:
A decorative public code formed from a fixed prefix and a Digital Persona's numeric identifier.
_Avoid_: Persona ID, listing ID

**Social Connection**:
A named family member or friend recorded as text within a Digital Persona. It does not reference another Digital Persona.
_Avoid_: Linked persona, relationship graph

**Characteristic**:
A structured fact about a Digital Persona that can later participate in filtering.
_Avoid_: Attribute, metadata, property
